// The agent pipeline

ONE PIPELINE.
END-TO-END.

BuildAgentOS runs a coordinated 6-agent pipeline — from finding your next customer to collecting their final payment. Every agent hands off to the next automatically.

01

Lead Qualifier — Decide What's Worth Time

Scores every incoming lead 0–100 on urgency, description completeness, and contact channels. Checks service area and a 30-day duplicate guard. Score 70+ auto-advances to the Estimator. Scores 50–69 go to manual review. Duplicates or out-of-area leads are logged, not silently dropped.

Lead scoringService-area checkDuplicate guardAuto-routing
02

Estimator — Build the Quote

Analyzes project details and photos using Claude, prices materials with a contingency buffer, applies your labor rate, overhead, and margin, and scores its own confidence. High confidence and a total under $10K sends the quote automatically. Over $50K always comes back to you for review. Over $250K is blocked outright.

AI-powered analysisMaterial pricingConfidence scoringAuto-proposal
03

Scheduler — Crew Dispatch

Finds the nearest crew with the right skill and actual capacity — reading real bookings out of the database, not just checking a calendar. Emergency jobs get same-day priority; standard jobs are slotted within 4 business days.

Capacity-awareSkill matchingEmergency priorityAuto-dispatch
04

Execution — Site Guardrails

Tracks jobs to completion. Change orders over $500 need approval. Won't flip a job to "completed" without a customer signature and at least 2 photos. Safety incidents trigger a critical stop with immediate owner notification.

Photo verificationChange-order gatingSafety stopCompletion tracking
05

Compliance — Per-Jurisdiction Checks

Checks permit thresholds by city, crew license validity, and insurance currency for that specific job's jurisdiction. An expired license is a hard block, not a warning.

Permit thresholdsLicense validationInsurance checksHard blocks
06

Billing — Paid, Only When Done

Refuses to generate an invoice until the Execution agent confirms the job is verifiably complete — signature and photos on file. Once confirmed, generates the invoice automatically and processes payment via Stripe.

Completion-gatedAuto-invoicingStripe
